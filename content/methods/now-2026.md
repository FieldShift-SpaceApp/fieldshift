**What it is.** The rain of the current wheat harvest window at the site point, from September 1, 2026 to the last day each source has a value (the cut-off requested from the APIs is November 13, 2026). Per source: total, number of days with **50 mm or more** (same threshold as the heavy-rain signal), wettest day, and the total of each month so far. The same calendar days are then read in past years: 2023 (the last El Niño winter before 2026) and the mean and median per ENSO phase over 1998-2025.

**Sources.** **NASA POWER** `PRECTOTCORR` (bias-corrected MERRA-2 precipitation, local solar time days), the only source that covers the whole window. **GPM IMERG** (NASA), served by the POWER API as `IMERG_PRECTOT` in UTC days, which at access stopped on 2026-09-14. The NASA GES DISC copies of IMERG (Early, Late and Final runs) need an Earthdata login, which this pipeline does not use.

**Phase.** Past years: NOAA CPC operational rule on the ONI (≥ +0.5 °C or ≤ −0.5 °C for at least five consecutive overlapping seasons), phase of the SON season, as in [heavy rain by ENSO phase](#enso-heavy-rain). 2026: the latest published season (ASO 2026), since SON 2026 is not complete.

**How we compute it.** `fieldshift complements` reads the raw caches of the current season (`data/cache/now-2026/`), the long POWER and IMERG caches for the past years and the ONI file, and writes `now-2026.json`. Every past year of 1998-2025 must have a value on each of those days; if one does not, the run stops instead of averaging fewer years. Month means are over all past years, phase means over the years of each phase.

**Reported, kept apart.** The `reported` block quotes the Emater/RS-Ascar crop bulletin (publisher, date, URL, page) on the 2026 wheat in Rio Grande do Sul. These statements are estimates made during the harvest; FieldShift does not turn them into a measured loss.
