## 1. Official limits (disqualifying if broken)

Source: [Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) and [Judging & Awards Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/), versions of 2026-11-13, read 2026-11-14.

| Rule | Official text | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Format | "Video (30 seconds or less) or slide deck (up to seven slides including title and intro slides)." | one of the two, not both |
| Hard limit | "Adhere to the 30-second and 7-slide limits. Projects that exceed these limits will not be eligible for Global Judging." | video: `ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration -of csv=p=0 demo.mp4` ≤ 30.0 (aim for ≤ 28 s); slides: count every slide, title and intro included, ≤ 7 |
| Language | Slide decks must be in English. A video may be spoken in another language, but "it MUST include English language subtitles." | burn the English captions into the frames (do not rely on platform auto-captions) |
| Link | "should not require a password, permission, or registration" | open in a private window, logged out, and on a phone. YouTube: "Unlisted" or "Public". Google Drive: "Anyone with the link — Viewer". Never "Restricted" |
| Upload | The project page does not accept video or document uploads | host the file and paste the link in field 3 |
| Minors | "may not include any images, video, audio, or other likeness to persons under 18 years old" | check every frame, photo and voice |
| Weight | "the majority of judges' evaluation will be based on your project submission"; "a strong project demo can increase the potential for a high score in the Presentation category" | Presentation is one of five criteria, scored 1–5 |

## 2. SI (AI) content rules

Official text (Project Submission Guide, "Use of SI"):
- "Generated content may not use, contain, or modify any NASA branding or design elements (such as the NASA logos, flags, or mission identifiers)."
- "Images and videos: Must include a visible watermark to indicate SI generation."
- "Audio: Must acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and metadata."
- "judges will be able to see any SI-generated indicators within your project [...] They will evaluate originality, intent, and execution based on the SI-assisted elements you include."

What this means for the demo video of a team member that uses a **synthetic voice** and **illustrative cards**:

| Element | Required label | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic (SI) narration voice | acknowledged in text **and** metadata | (1) video description: "Narration voice is AI-generated (`<tool>`)."; (2) file metadata, e.g. `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -metadata comment="Narration voice is AI-generated (<tool>)" -codec copy out.mp4`; (3) a short on-screen caption at the start (recommended: judges see SI indicators); (4) a row in `SI-USAGE.md`; (5) field 6 and field 8 of the page |
| Cards, images or frames generated by SI | visible watermark | "AI-generated" in a corner of every such card, readable at the size shown, for its whole time on screen |
| Illustrative cards that do not show real data (SI or not) | label "Illustrative" | on the card itself. Repo rule (AGENTS.md §2.5): every number shown as data comes from code that reads a cited dataset; an illustrative card must not look like a result |
| Screen recordings of the real app | none needed | numbers come from NASA POWER through the pipeline; keep the provenance visible if possible |
| Any image, icon or music not made by the team | source + license | field 8 of the page |

Also: no NASA logo ("meatball", "worm"), flag or mission patch anywhere in the demo, SI-generated or not (repo rule C3); dataset names as plain text are fine. Do not synthesize the voice or face of a real person without their consent, and never of anyone under 18.
