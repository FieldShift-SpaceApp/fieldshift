What's still needed

  1. Soil analysis of the 0-20 cm and 20-40 cm layers by an accredited laboratory (V%, Ca, m%; valid 24 months; IN 2/2025 items 4.1-III and 7)
  2. Confirmation of contour sowing (or sowing on the level) on at least 75% of the area where the slope is 3% or more (item 3.2.1-I)
  3. The farmer's declaration of crops and species of the last three agricultural years (item 4.1-I and II); it can raise diversity to level 4 and corrects what the satellite misreads
  4. Registration of the field by an accredited agent so that Embrapa's SINM issues the official level (IN 2/2025 items 3, 4.2-4.3); useful soil depth (Portaria SPA/MAPA 209/2026 item 2.4)
